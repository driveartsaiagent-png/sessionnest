SessionNest – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 7, 2026

Summary

SessionNest does not collect, sell or share any personal data. Everything you save stays on your own computer.

What is stored, and where

To do its job, SessionNest reads the addresses (URLs), titles and tab-group names of your open tabs and saves them in the extension's local storage on your device (chrome.storage.local). This data is never sent to us or to anyone else. You can delete any session at any time; uninstalling the extension deletes all of its data.

If you turn on daily backups (Pro), a backup file is written to your own Downloads folder.

Network requests

SessionNest makes no network requests, with one exception: when you activate a Pro license, the license key is sent to Gumroad (api.gumroad.com) to check that it is valid, and it is re-checked about once a week. Gumroad's privacy policy applies to your purchase: gumroad.com/privacy.

No tracking

No analytics, no advertising, no cookies, no fingerprinting, no remote code.
