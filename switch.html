Switch from OneTab, Session Buddy or Tab Session Manager

If an update, a restart or a crash ever wiped your saved tabs, you know the feeling. SessionNest is a session manager built so that doesn't happen: it saves on its own every few minutes and after every change, keeps a history of snapshots, and offers to bring everything back after a crash.

Moving your saved tabs takes about a minute. Nothing is uploaded anywhere — the import happens on your computer.

1. Install SessionNest (free)

  • Chrome Web Store — Chrome, Brave, Opera, Vivaldi
  • Microsoft Edge Add-ons

2. Bring your tabs over

From OneTab

  1. Open OneTab and click Export / Import URLs.
  2. Copy all the text in the export box.
  3. In SessionNest, open the manager → Settings → Import, paste the text and click Import. Each group of links becomes its own window.

From Session Buddy or Tab Session Manager

  1. Use the extension's export or backup option to save a .json file.
  2. In SessionNest: Settings → Import → choose the file.

A plain list of web addresses (one per line) works too.
